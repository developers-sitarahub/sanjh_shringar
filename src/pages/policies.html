Size & measurement guide

Every outfit can be made in a standard size or, at no extra cost, to your exact measurements. Measure over the undergarments you'll wear on the day, and keep the tape snug but not tight.

Shipping & delivery

  • Made-to-order: dispatched in about 3 weeks from measurement confirmation.
  • Ready-to-ship: dispatched within 3–5 working days.
  • Delivery: tracked shipping across India, usually 4–7 working days after dispatch.
  • International: available on request. Message us for a quote.
  • Urgent orders: message us before ordering and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Exchanges & alterations

  • Made-to-measure pieces are made only for you, so they can't be returned or refunded.
  • If something doesn't fit as it should, or has a manufacturing defect, we'll alter or fix it free of charge. Please tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.
  • Ready-to-ship and sale items in standard sizes can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery if they're unused, unwashed and have their tags intact.
  • Slight colour variation from screen to fabric, and small irregularities in handwork, are part of handmade craft and aren't considered defects.

Payments

We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and wallets through a secure, encrypted payment gateway. We never see or store your card details.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order: your contact details, address, measurements and payment confirmation. We never sell your data. You can ask us to view, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to sanjhshringar.shopify@gmail.com.

Terms

Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and include applicable taxes unless stated. Product photos are of our real pieces, but handmade items may vary slightly. By placing an order you agree to these policies.
